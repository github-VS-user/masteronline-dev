Rules & Tutorial

Rules

By playing, you agree to follow these rules. Violations result in bans.

No hacking/cheating

Using game modifications or external software (aimbot, ESP, exploits, etc.) for a competitive advantage is prohibited.

No teaming

If you're not on the same team, you're enemies. Helping other players, sharing items, or colluding is not allowed.

No inappropriate usernames

Usernames with slurs, hateful language, or hack advertisements are prohibited.

VPN/proxy policy

VPNs and proxies are blocked to prevent ban evasion.

Tutorial

New player? Here's how to play.

Controls

Movement - WASD or Arrow Keys

Aim - Mouse

Attack - Left-Click

Change weapons - 1-4 or Scroll Wheel

Pick up items / Interact / Revive - F

Reload - R

Use healing items - Click or 7-0

Cancel action - X

Toggle map - G or M

Toggle minimap - N

Drop item - Right-click

Equip last item - Q

Swap gun slots - T

Equip other gun - Space

Keybinds can be changed in settings!

How to Play

Be the last player standing. Loot weapons, armor, and supplies. Fight other players. Avoid the deadly orange gas that shrinks the safe zone.
